About Us

A Trusted Partner in Construction & Development

Founded in 2018, Mpahane Group (Pty) Ltd is a proudly 100% black-owned enterprise headquartered in Kwa-Thema, Springs. We specialise in construction, civil works, project management and facility management — delivering quality outcomes that uplift communities and empower clients.

Our mission goes beyond bricks and mortar: we believe in creating sustainable employment, fighting poverty, and building the South Africa we want to see. Every project we undertake carries that purpose.

Our Process

How We Deliver Your Project

A clear, structured approach that keeps you informed and in control from first call to final handover.

1

Consultation & Quote

Contact us for a free consultation. We listen to your needs, assess the scope, and provide a clear, itemised quotation with no hidden fees.

2

Planning & Design

Our team develops a detailed project plan, timeline and resource schedule. For builds, we provide architectural guidance aligned with SACAP standards.

3

Execution

Work begins on site with our skilled teams. You receive regular progress updates, and our project managers ensure quality control at every stage.

4

Handover & Support

After thorough inspection and sign-off, we hand over a completed project. We remain available for any after-care support or follow-on work.
